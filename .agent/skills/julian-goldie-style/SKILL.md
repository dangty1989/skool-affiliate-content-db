# Julian Goldie Style Guide

## Purpose
This skill transforms standard, academic, or "AI-sounding" content into the persuasive, high-energy, and viral-ready style of Julian Goldie. It is optimized for building an "Affiliate Machine" and maximizing conversions to Skool communities.

## When to Use
- **Processing NotebookLM Outputs**: When a Video/Audio Overview sounds too academic or robotic.
- **Writing Video Scripts**: For TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Reels.
- **Crafting SEO Content**: Blog posts and landing pages focused on "The Machine."
- **Social Media Distribution**: High-impact posts for LinkedIn and X.

---

## 1. The Voice & Tone
- **High Energy & Authority**: You've "solved the system." Use phrases like "I found the secret," "This is a game-changer," "Stop doing [X]."
- **Specific Results**: Never say "good revenue." Say "$47,329 in 30 days." Specific numbers build massive trust.
- **Brevity**: Short, punchy sentences. One idea per line.
- **Conversational**: Write like you're talking to a friend over a coffee—but a friend who is obsessed with ROI.

---

## 2. The Julian Hook Formulas
The first 3 seconds are everything. Julian uses "Pattern Interrupts" and "Massive Value" promises:

- **The Mistake Hook**: "Stop wasting time on [Common Practice]. It's killing your business. Do this instead..."
- **The Secret Hook**: "Nobody is talking about how AI is actually making money right now. I just built a machine that..."
- **The Result Hook**: "$10,000 using nothing but 100% free AI tools. Here's exactly how I did it (Step-by-step)."
- **The Controversial Hook**: "SEO is NOT dead. You're just doing it wrong."

---

## 3. Transforming NotebookLM Video Overviews
NotebookLM creates a "Deep Dive" but it's often a polite conversation. Julian would strip the politeness and keep the **Gold**.

### Step 1: Extract the "Gold Nuggets"
Find the 3 most shocking or valuable facts in the Video Overview.
- *Academic*: "The document discusses 7 steps for affiliate success."
- *Julian Style*: "I found the 7-step blueprint Julian Goldie used to dominate the affiliate game."

### Step 2: Apply the "Direct Response" Filter
Remove filler words (*"In conclusion," "It's important to note," "Essentially"*).
Replace with transition words that drive momentum (*"Wait," "Check this out," "Here's the catch"*).

### Step 3: Format for "The Machine" (F.Y.W.H.E.E.L)
Structure the transformed script to lead toward a CTA:
1. **HOOK**: Shocking fact or result.
2. **THE PROBLEM**: Why current methods fail.
3. **THE SOLUTION**: Your AI-powered insight from the Notebook.
4. **THE PROOF**: Specific numbers or "The Machine" concept.
5. **CTA (The Wire)**: "Join my Skool community to get the full workflow."


## 5. Key Keywords to Include
*   **The Machine**: Your automated business system.
*   **SEO Dominance**: Winning Google search.
*   **Autopilot**: Running with zero effort once set up.
*   **Game-changer**: A revolutionary tool or tactic.
*   **Blueprint**: A proven, step-by-step plan.
